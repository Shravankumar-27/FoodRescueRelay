RESCUERELAY — SHORT PROJECT NOTES

RescueRelay connects surplus-food donors with nearby NGOs, community kitchens
and shelters before the pickup deadline. The pilot focuses on Hyderabad
caterers and event venues, using synthetic demonstration data.

FEATURES AVAILABLE
- Quick surplus posting, saved drafts and repeat donations.
- Matching by distance, pickup time, capacity, dietary needs and allergens.
- Role-specific maps, nearby partners, favourites and community rankings.
- Claims, pickup codes, live status, expiry handling and delivery confirmation.
- Private chat, suggested messages and food-split proposals.
- Container/transport coordination and supporting logistics providers.
- Receiver capacity settings and a food allocation / packing planner.
- Meal feedback, partner ratings and transparent trust scores.
- Personal/community impact: confirmed meals, estimated food weight and CO2e,
  with assumptions and downloadable reports.
- Kindness coins, medals, activity streaks and contribution history.
- Individual appreciation posters plus overall lifetime appreciation posters;
  donor/receiver artwork, two themes, PNG download and print/PDF.
- Admin partner verification, rescue oversight, certificate management,
  logistics provider management and audit records.
- Responsive layouts, simple Back navigation and focused error messages.

DEMO HIGHLIGHT
Madhapur Grand Events and Hope Community Kitchen have synthetic completed
delivery history earning Community champion, the highest contribution medal.
10 coins = 1 confirmed delivered meal; the top medal starts at 50,000 coins.

BUSINESS IDEA
Donors could pay for coordination tools and impact reporting; sponsors could
fund transport and neighbourhood coverage. Pricing is a proposal; billing
and paid benefits are not active. Receiving food stays accessible.

IMPORTANT DISTINCTIONS
Meals are servings, not unique people. Climate/food-weight figures are
estimates. Coins are recognition points with no cash value. The platform
does not certify food safety or promise tax/legal benefits.
